    About the taxonomy

    The list follows the survey From Trajectories to Experience: A Survey of Experience-Driven LLM Agents (preprint coming soon). Agent experience is action-guiding information grounded in task-execution trajectories and retained to guide later episodes where it applies: a trajectory kept as a precedent, a semantic lesson, or a reusable procedure.

    • Formation selects or derives candidate experience from trajectories.
    • Organization makes it accessible.
    • Utilization applies it to current decisions or internalizes it into model parameters.
    • Maintenance admits, consolidates, revises, and retires it as evidence accumulates.

    Notes on each entry summarize how the survey characterizes the work. Systems the survey marks as adjacent (general or conversational memory outside its experience scope) say so in their notes.
